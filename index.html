Angel AI – Privacy Policy

Operated by Angel AI (by Fr. Arockia Selvakumar SDB) • Last Updated: October 2025

1. Introduction

Angel AI is a spiritual companion powered by artificial intelligence to assist users with reflection, prayer, liturgy preparation, and faith-based inspiration. This Privacy Policy explains how user data is handled.

2. No Data Collection or Storage

Angel AI does not collect, store, or share any personal information outside the ChatGPT platform. All interactions occur within OpenAI’s secure infrastructure and are subject to OpenAI’s privacy standards.

3. Use of Third-Party Integrations (e.g., Google Drive, Blogger)

If Angel AI accesses external content such as Smart Liturgy Google Drive or Blog materials, it is only upon the user’s request (e.g., “Find Mission Sunday liturgy from Drive”). No user data is stored or logged from such interactions.

4. No Cookies, Tracking, or Analytics

Angel AI does not use cookies, trackers, analytics systems, or profiling tools of any kind.

5. User Responsibility

Users are advised not to share sensitive personal, medical, financial, or confidential information within the conversation. Angel AI is designed for inspiration and support, not for emergency, legal, or medical services.

6. AI-Generated Content Disclaimer

Responses from Angel AI are AI-generated and may occasionally contain inaccuracies. Users are encouraged to verify scriptural or doctrinal content with authoritative sources before public use.

7. Contact & Ownership

Angel AI is maintained under the initiative: Angel AI (by Fr. Arockia Selvakumar SDB).

For inquiries, contact via Smart Liturgy channels.

8. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be revised periodically to maintain compliance with evolving standards. Continued use of Angel AI implies acceptance of the most recent version.
